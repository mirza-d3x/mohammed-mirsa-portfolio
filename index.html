Background

About Me

I am a Software Engineer with over four years of comprehensive experience designing, developing, and maintaining mobile and web applications. My background spans cross-platform engineering with Flutter and Dart, native Android development with Java and Kotlin, and native iOS development with Objective-C.

Much of my professional work has been concentrated in domain-heavy environments—fintech, digital banking, securities trading, and enterprise CRM/POS systems. These domains demand zero-tolerance for data inconsistencies, strict compliance with national identity and payment rails (such as UAE Pass and Aani), and resilient offline-first architectures.

Throughout my career, I have stepped into critical technical roles: from architecting high-security remittance systems and mentoring engineering teams at M2H Infotech, to taking sole operational ownership of production trading applications at Geojit Technologies following team departures. I view software engineering not merely as writing widgets, but as understanding product requirements thoroughly, making sensible architectural decisions, and ensuring reliable application behavior throughout its lifecycle.
